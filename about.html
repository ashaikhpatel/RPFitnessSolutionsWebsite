About Us

Chicago's Fitness Equipment Repair Specialists

RP Fitness Solutions is a locally owned, owner-operated service based in Niles, IL. We specialise in the repair, maintenance and assembly of all types of fitness equipment — for homeowners, condo buildings, corporate gyms and commercial facilities across Chicago and the surrounding suburbs.

Every job is handled personally by our certified technician. No subcontractors, no call centres — just direct, professional service with a 90-day warranty on every repair and a firm policy of supplying parts at cost, with no markup.

Our Principles

How We Work

Three things guide every repair, every visit, every customer interaction.

💰

Parts at Cost

We source every part at our cost and pass that price directly to you. No markup, no inflated parts bills. When we quote you a part price, it's exactly what we paid for it.

🛡️

90-Day Warranty

Every repair we do is backed by a 90-day warranty. If the same issue occurs within 90 days, we return and fix it at no charge — no questions, no arguments.

🔍

Honest Diagnosis

We tell you exactly what's wrong and exactly what it costs to fix — before we do anything. If a repair isn't worth doing, we'll tell you that too.
